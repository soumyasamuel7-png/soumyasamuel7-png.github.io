Welcome! I'm starting this blog to document real lessons from the data governance work I do day to day — metadata management, lineage, access policy design, and the platform tradeoffs that come with tools like Collibra, Purview, and Immuta.

## What to expect

- Field notes from platform implementations
- Breakdowns of governance frameworks and how they hold up in practice
- Occasional deep dives into data lineage and cataloging patterns

Thanks for reading, and feel free to reach out if any of this is useful for what you're building.
